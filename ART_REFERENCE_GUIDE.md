# original pocketmal drawing references

these uploaded paper sheets are the owner-approved source references for the creature art pass. they are currently chat attachments, not files stored in this repository; do not claim that they have been committed as image assets.

## design rules

- preserve the exact species identity, silhouette, facial features, appendages, markings, and evolution relationships from the drawings.
- don't substitute generic procedural designs or invent details to fill unclear areas.
- the pencil labels are sometimes faint or overlapping. treat any uncertain reading below as tentative and ask the owner before using it as a final species name.
- create clean pixel art from the drawings; don't trace pencil noise or photograph shadows.
- human sprites use exaggerated chibi proportions: head roughly 50–60% of height, compact torso, short limbs, tiny feet, expressive readable face. this applies to the player, rival/friends, professor lotus, gym leaders, elite four, champion, team meridian, trainers, and npcs.
- pocketmals retain their own species anatomy and are not humanized.

## sheet inventory (visual, not a verified roster)

1. **starter concepts and evolution sketches** — the sheet has the three starter families and notes about first/second/third stages; visible labels include kangol, bubbish, lizrout, and names resembling flareroo / foamish / thornko. confirm any faint handwriting before committing filenames.
2. **large evolution sketches** — visible names include branack, flaresupial, and jelluburst. the designs show the larger evolved forms; preserve their distinct head shapes, horns/ears, shell/cloud-like forms, and body silhouettes.
3. **species concept sheet** — includes several distinct small and large creature concepts, including a bird-like creature, a compact nestling, a spiky/rocky creature, and a floating cloud-like creature. some labels are hard to read in the photo, so use the original image for identification rather than guessing.
4. **additional character concepts** — two large sketches with labels that appear to read lovlittle and malittle. verify from the owner before assigning names.
5. **forest-route species** — labels visible include grainper, wheafield, rascoon, and racrash; notes indicate bug/grass and normal/normal or normal/dark concepts. keep the family designs visually related without making their silhouettes identical.
6. **bird concepts and evolution notes** — several bird stages and notes about feather/crest shapes, talons, and species inspirations. use the actual sketches as reference; avoid turning them into generic birds.
